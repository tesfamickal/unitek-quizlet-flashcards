One-page mental model

Lymphatic system

Moves lymph back to blood, filters pathogens, and uses lymphocytes. High-yield organs: lymph nodes, tonsils, spleen, thymus, appendix, bone marrow.

Immunity

Passive = antibodies received from another source, like breast milk. Active/acquired = body builds response after disease exposure or vaccination.

Infections

Viruses: influenza, herpes zoster/shingles, rabies, HIV, Ebola, mumps. Fungus: athlete’s foot. Parasite: malaria, toxoplasmosis.

Cancer terms

Benign is usually not life-threatening; malignant is cancerous/life-threatening. Metastasis/metastasize = cancer spreads to new site.
