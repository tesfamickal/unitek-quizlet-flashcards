Q1Worked dosage item

8 lb 4 oz to kg

Show answer
Answer: 8 lb 4 oz = 8.25 lb. 8.25 ÷ 2.2 = 3.75 kg.

Carry units through every step; unit errors are safety errors.

Q2Worked dosage item

1 L at 50 mL/hr started 6:30 AM; now 9 AM; next bag?

Show answer
Answer: 1000 ÷ 50 = 20 hr total. 6:30 AM + 20 hr = 2:30 AM next day.

Carry units through every step; unit errors are safety errors.

Q3Worked dosage item

125 mg ordered; 62.5 mg/5 mL; convert to teaspoons

Show answer
Answer: 125 ÷ 62.5 = 2. 2 × 5 mL = 10 mL. 10 ÷ 5 = 2 tsp.

Carry units through every step; unit errors are safety errors.

Q4Worked dosage item

0.6 g ordered; 100 mg/5 mL; convert to fluid ounces

Show answer
Answer: 0.6 g = 600 mg. 600 ÷ 100 = 6. 6 × 5 mL = 30 mL = 1 fl oz.

Carry units through every step; unit errors are safety errors.

Q7Worked dosage item

Show answer
Answer: 400 cm = 4 m. 288 + 4 = 292 m. 292 ÷ 1000 = 0.292 km ≈ 0.3 km.

Carry units through every step; unit errors are safety errors.

Q8Worked dosage item

6 ft 5 in to m

Show answer
Answer: 6 ft 5 in = 77 in. 77 × 2.54 = 195.58 cm = 1.9558 m. The quiz accepted 1.9 m; standard tenths rounding is 2.0 m, so follow course answer if retaking.

Carry units through every step; unit errors are safety errors.
